# Bio

I am the founder of Boring Research Lab, where we're cooking up some interesting stuff for education and AI. Previously, I was an NLP research assistant in the [Gaelic Algorithmic Research Group](https://gaelic.algresearch.org/) at the University of Edinburgh. I was also fortunate to be a research assistant in the [Samarajiwa's Lab](https://samarajiwa-lab.org/) at MRC Cancer Unit, the University of Cambridge.

I have a broad interest in developing machine learning and deep learning models on multi-omics datasets, medial images, and AI Agents. I aim to develop machine learning models and automated systems to make the world better.

## Research at Edinburgh

Developing a deep learning model for Gaelic Spelling Correction. The major challenge is due to the lack of data in this Low-Resource Language. We overcame this problem by using some data augmentation tricks.

## Research at Cam (computational biology)

Apply machine learning and deep learning on genomics data to build gene regulatory networks in the immune system. I tried to build an SS-ZOO for sslab. There were FROGS, [COBRA](https://github.com/xihajun/COBRA), and other animals, and hopefully there would be more to come in the future.
